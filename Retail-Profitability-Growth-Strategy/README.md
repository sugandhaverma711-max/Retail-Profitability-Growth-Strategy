# Retail Profitability & Growth Strategy

> **End-to-End Business Intelligence & Consulting Portfolio Project**

An end-to-end business analytics project that simulates a real-world consulting engagement for **RetailMart**, a fictional nationwide retail company. The objective is to identify why profitability has not kept pace with sales growth and provide data-driven recommendations using **Excel, Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI**.

---

## Business Problem

RetailMart has experienced consistent revenue growth but inconsistent profitability across products, customer segments, and regions. Management requires actionable insights to identify profit drivers, reduce losses, and support strategic decision-making.

---

## Project Objectives

- Identify key drivers of profitability
- Analyze sales, customers, products, and regional performance
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profit
- Develop an executive Power BI dashboard
- Deliver strategic business recommendations

## Business Insights

- Identified profitable and loss-making products.
- Compared performance across regions and customer segments.
- Evaluated the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Highlighted high-value customers and top-performing categories.
- Delivered recommendations to improve long-term profitability.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Optimize discount strategy.
- Prioritize high-profit product lines.
- Improve performance in underperforming regions.
- Focus on retaining high-value customers.
- Monitor executive KPIs through interactive dashboards.
